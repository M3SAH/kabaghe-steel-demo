About Kabaghe Steel & Fabricators

We provide custom steel fabrication solutions for residential and commercial customers across Lusaka. From enhancing the security of your property to building robust structural supports, our work is defined by strength and precision.

Our capabilities include custom gates, sliding window frames, burglar bars, steel doors, tank stands, and bespoke fabricated steel structures.
